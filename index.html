Frequently asked questions

The app doesn't count my reps.
Make sure the camera sees your full body, stand 2–3 meters away and ensure the room is well lit. For squats, the camera should see your legs from the side. You can also try switching the tracked side (left/right) from your profile or settings.

The counter is inaccurate or counts extra reps.
Counting is based on your body pose, so loose clothing, occlusions and poor lighting can affect accuracy. Slow, controlled movements give the best results.

Reminders don't arrive.
Check that notifications are allowed for RepNexo in your device settings, and that reminders are enabled in the app's Settings screen. Then restart the app — it re-schedules reminders on launch.

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, tap “Forgot password” (if available) or email us and we will help you restore access to your account.

How do I delete my account?
Email us at alexmorozov021294@gmail.com from the address registered in the app. We will delete your account and all associated data (profile, workout history, stats, avatar) within 30 days.

Is my camera video uploaded anywhere?
No. Camera frames are processed entirely on your device to detect your pose. No video or photos ever leave your phone.
