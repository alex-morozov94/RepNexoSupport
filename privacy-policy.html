RepNexo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Overview

RepNexo (“the App”) is a workout counter that uses your device camera to count squats and push-ups and keeps your streaks, history and ranking in a cloud account. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, why, and what rights you have.

By using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Data we collect

Account data: your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), and — if you use Sign in with Apple — the name and email address you share with us through Apple.

Profile data: your nickname and an optional avatar photo that you upload from your photo library.

Workout data: dates of your workouts, the exercise type, the number of repetitions counted, and the streaks and leaderboard statistics derived from them.

Preferences: your language, default exercise, daily goal and reminder settings.

Camera and pose processing

The camera is used only to detect your body pose and count repetitions. Video frames are processed entirely on your device. The App does not record, store or upload video or photos from the camera, and no camera frames ever leave your device.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push-notification tokens or notification content are sent to or stored on our servers.

Third-party services

Supabase (supabase.com) — cloud database and file storage where your account, profile, workout data and avatar are stored, protected by row-level security so that only you can access your own records.

Apple (apple.com) — only if you choose Sign in with Apple; governed by Apple’s own privacy policy.

The App contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics and no tracking tools. We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone for marketing purposes.

Data security and retention

Data is transferred over HTTPS and stored in a database protected by row-level security policies. Your data is kept for as long as your account exists and is deleted when you delete your account.

Your rights

You can view and correct your data (nickname, avatar, settings) directly in the App. You can export or delete all of your data by deleting your account — contact us at the email below and we will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available in the App’s Settings, with the effective date shown at the top.
